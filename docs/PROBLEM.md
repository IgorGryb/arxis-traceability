# Problem, audience and success criteria

**Problem.** Can requirements, decisions, document versions and artifacts for a sample residential project be traced and checked for completeness?

**Audience.** Architecture or project team that needs reviewable decision provenance.

**Success criterion.** The demonstrator produces a traceability matrix, detects a deliberately missing link and labels documents with an explainable AI-assist proxy.

Out of scope: production deployment, client outcomes, regulatory approval and any claim not present in the evidence manifest.
