# Hypotheses, prediction and falsification

## Primary hypothesis

The demonstrator produces a traceability matrix, detects a deliberately missing link and labels documents with an explainable AI-assist proxy.

## Prediction recorded before test

The complete fixture will reach full requirement coverage and the negative fixture will be detected as incomplete.

## Falsification criterion

The prediction is falsified if the complete fixture has uncovered requirements or the deliberately broken fixture passes completeness checks.

## Alternative explanation

A passing result may follow from the deliberately narrow fixture or chosen assumptions rather than general capability. External validity therefore remains unconfirmed.
