## Layers to develop

### Business

Select one project participant and one document-control pain point. Quantify rework, response time, missing-decision risk and coordination cost; define who pays and why traceability changes their workflow.

### Technology

Develop a versioned document and requirement graph, immutable provenance events, role-based access, change-impact queries, completeness rules and a human-reviewed classifier. Keep CAD/BIM/GIS as explicit interfaces, not implemented claims.

### Operations

Model requirements, design decisions, RFIs, submittals, approvals, revisions, change orders, issue closure and handover. Assign owner, SLA and evidence at every state transition.

### Finance Risk

Connect document completeness to schedule delay, rework, contingency and financing risk. Use ranges and scenarios, not invented savings.

### Governance

Define authoritative document versions, approval rights, retention, access, audit trails, professional accountability and conditions under which AI suggestions must be rejected.

3. Test missing approvals, superseded documents, conflicting revisions and unauthorized access.
4. Estimate rework/delay exposure with explicit construction-finance assumptions.
5. Run a completeness review with an architecture or construction practitioner.
